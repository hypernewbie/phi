Phi Themes for Vim & Neovim

Installation:
- Vim: Copy .vim files to ~/.vim/colors/ (or %USERPROFILE%\vimfiles\colors\ on Windows)
- Neovim: Copy .vim files to ~/.config/nvim/colors/ (or %LOCALAPPDATA%\nvim\colors\ on Windows)

Usage:
In your vimrc or init.vim / init.lua:
:colorscheme phi_cyan (or any other phi_* color)
